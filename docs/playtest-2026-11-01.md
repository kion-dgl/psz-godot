## Rebuild and verify

The generated mirrors and audio are asset-pack content, not vendored SEGA assets.
With the existing local asset library installed:

```sh
python3 scripts/tools/prepare_playtest_audio.py
godot --headless --path . --editor --import --quit
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/stage_mirror_bake/underground.gd
godot --headless --path . --export-pack 'Asset Pack' /tmp/psz-playtest-assets.pck
```

Run `res://scripts/tools/playtest_feedback_probe.tscn` with
`PSZ_PROBE_PACK=/tmp/psz-playtest-assets.pck` in an isolated project/user-data
namespace. The probe creates a test character, so do not run it on the live save.
It checks shop geometry, audio guards, turret physics, flyer anchoring, native
materials, actual EXP, and explicit/fallback E/B entry directions.
The standard `res://scripts/tools/test_runner.tscn` includes seeded entry tests.

Publication remains separate: the new pack must be uploaded and
`assets_manifest.json` / `asset_tree.txt` regenerated together before release.
The existing published manifest is intentionally retained during local testing.

Scorecard areas reviewed: City (`score:city`), Combat (`score:combat`),
Traps (`score:traps`), Free Roam (`score:free-roam`) and HUD (`score:hud`). Grades and assessment dates are
unchanged; owner playtesting is pending.
